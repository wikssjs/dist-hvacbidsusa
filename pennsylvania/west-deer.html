What size AC system do I need for my West Deer home?
Proper AC sizing is critical — oversized systems short-cycle and cause humidity problems; undersized systems can't keep up on hot PA days. The correct method is a Manual J load calculation performed by your licensed West Deer HVAC contractor, which accounts for your home's square footage, insulation, window area, ceiling height, and local climate data. As a rough guide: 1 ton per 600-1,000 sq ft, but don't let a contractor quote based on square footage alone without a proper load calculation.

Permits & regulations in Pennsylvania

In Pennsylvania, HVAC replacements and new installations are typically not just a “swap it and go” project. Contractors doing the work must be properly registered and the job must be done with correct refrigerant handling and code-compliant installation. As a homeowner in West Deer, you should expect these requirements to come up before the crew starts.

First, Pennsylvania requires HVAC contractors to hold a Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration through the Pennsylvania Attorney General’s Office. For gas appliances, the installing technician must hold an active PA gas piping license. For refrigerant work on AC and heat pumps, the technician must have EPA Section 608 certification—ask for the technician’s EPA 608 card. Mechanical permits are required by most municipalities for HVAC replacement and new installation; a permit is typically not required for like-for-like filter changes or thermostat swaps. Verify the exact permit rules with your township or borough before the start date.

For rebates, check the PA Homeowner Rebate program and utility rebates (PPL Electric, PECO, Columbia Gas) for qualifying high-efficiency heat pumps and furnaces tied to federal IRA Inflation Reduction Act programs. ENERGY STAR certified equipment may also qualify for federal tax credits up to 30% under Section 25C. Equipment and your income-eligible documentation can matter.

To verify a contractor, check the PA HIC registration at hic.attorneygeneral.gov, request proof of EPA 608 certification from the technician, and review certificate of insurance plus workers’ compensation documentation. Then confirm in writing what permits will be pulled for your address and what inspections are expected.

How to choose a HVAC installation contractor in West Deer

Picking the right installer in West Deer is about more than a low quote—it’s about credentials, workmanship, and how clearly the company explains what it will (and won’t) do. Start by verifying IICRC certification where applicable to the service being performed and confirm Pennsylvania licensing. For contractor registration and accountability, check the PA HIC listing online. For HVAC technicians, ask for proof of EPA Section 608 certification if refrigerant handling is involved, and request documentation for gas piping licensing when installing or replacing gas equipment.

Next, get 2–3 itemized written quotes. The best estimates separate labor, materials, electrical work, ductwork changes, and any airflow/combustion testing or balancing so you can compare apples to apples. Also read the scope line by line: what’s excluded (old equipment disposal, duct sealing, electrical permits), what accessories are included, and how comfort is guaranteed (or measured). If the contractor mentions insurance claims, ask whether documentation for the claim process is included.

For warranty, confirm the workmanship warranty length in writing and distinguish it from equipment warranties. Payment schedules should be reasonable—never more than about 10–15% upfront. Hold back the remainder until the job is complete and you’ve confirmed operation, proper airflow, and clean termination of refrigerant/electrical connections. Finally, ask for a specific arrival estimate and a completion window in writing, including what happens if parts are backordered.
