Permits & regulations in Pennsylvania

In Pennsylvania, HVAC work is tied to licensing, contractor registration, and safe refrigerant handling requirements—so it’s worth verifying credentials before anyone starts cutting drywall. Pennsylvania requires HVAC contractors to hold a Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration through the Pennsylvania Attorney General’s Office. If gas appliances are involved (typical for furnace replacement or any gas-fired work), the installing technician must hold an active PA gas piping license.

For AC and heat pump systems that use refrigerant, technicians must have EPA Section 608 certification. When you schedule an estimate in Marple, ask to see the technician’s EPA 608 card. Mechanical permits are required by most PA municipalities for HVAC replacement and new installation; however, like-for-like filter changes and thermostat swaps typically don’t require a permit. Always confirm the specifics with your local Marple Township office or the relevant borough/township authority.

For financial incentives, check the PA Homeowner Rebate program and utility rebates through providers such as PPL Electric and PECO, as well as Columbia Gas where applicable, especially for qualifying high-efficiency heat pumps and furnaces tied to IRA Inflation Reduction Act programs. Also note: ENERGY STAR–certified equipment may qualify for federal tax credits up to 30% under Section 25C.

Marple homeowner checklist (step-by-step): (1) Verify the business’s HIC registration at hic.attorneygeneral.gov; (2) request the technician’s EPA 608 card; (3) ask for certificate of insurance and workers’ compensation proof; (4) confirm permits will be pulled for the scope (replacement/new installation). Keep copies—permits, equipment specs, and credentials should be part of your job file.

How to choose a HVAC installation contractor in Marple
